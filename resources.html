Why These Voices Matter

No single voice shaped this journey alone. Some sources approached Scripture through language and history. Others approached through theology, translation, archaeology, pastoral care, grief, identity, or lived experience inside wounded faith communities. Together, these voices reveal something important: honest study has never been the enemy of faith. The history of Scripture is the history of people wrestling carefully with sacred text across generations. Questions survived. Translation survived. Covenant survived. Tenderness survived. The conversation surrounding David and Jonathan survived because readers continued returning to the emotional honesty preserved within their story. These resources helped shape that journey.
