PRAYER RECEIVED

Your prayer matters here.

Thank you for trusting this space with something sacred. Some requests arrive carrying grief. Some carry exhaustion. Some carry fear that has remained unspoken for years. Some carry hope fragile enough to tremble while being written. Every prayer shared here is treated with care, dignity, compassion, and confidentiality.

You are not weak for needing prayer. You are not forgotten for feeling overwhelmed. You are not alone while carrying difficult things quietly. Jesus still moves gently toward wounded people.

Whether your request was shared publicly or privately, prayer will continue beyond this page. May peace breathe slowly back into your heart.

“Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest.”

Matthew 11:28
