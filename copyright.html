SCRIPTURE & TRANSLATION

Sacred Text & Interpretation

Unless otherwise noted, Scripture quotations throughout this manuscript are drawn primarily from the King James Version of the Holy Bible. Additional historical, linguistic, and scholarly observations may reference Hebrew terminology, translation history, Septuagint traditions, and interpretive scholarship across Jewish and Christian traditions.

This project approaches Scripture with reverence, historical curiosity, pastoral concern, and deep respect for the emotional complexity preserved within the biblical text itself.

RESEARCH STATEMENT

Educational & Reflective Purpose

This manuscript was created as a literary, theological, historical, and reflective exploration of covenant, tenderness, masculinity, grief, translation, fear, and interpretation within the story of David and Jonathan.

The work does not seek to erase Scripture, diminish faith, or force uniform conclusions upon readers. Rather, this project exists to encourage thoughtful study, emotional honesty, historical awareness, compassion toward wounded believers, and deeper engagement with sacred text.

Honest readers may ultimately arrive at different conclusions regarding David and Jonathan, covenant language, masculinity, emotional intimacy, or theological interpretation. Space for thoughtful disagreement remains part of the integrity of serious study itself.
