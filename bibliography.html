PRESERVED SOURCES

Bibliography

Every archive is shaped by memory, language, history, and the voices preserved before us. These works helped inform the study of covenant, translation, tenderness, grief, theology, and interpretation throughout this project.

“Some truths survive because generations refused to stop studying them.”

HEBREW LANGUAGE

Hebrew Word Studies & Ancient Language

Resources focused upon covenant language, Hebrew terminology, emotional expression within Scripture, and linguistic frameworks surrounding David and Jonathan.

Brown Driver Briggs Hebrew Lexicon

Foundational Hebrew lexicon widely used for Old Testament language study and covenant terminology.

The Hebrew and Aramaic Lexicon of the Old Testament

Advanced lexical resource examining Hebrew word meanings, semantic ranges, and contextual interpretation.

Theological Wordbook of the Old Testament

Extensive theological analysis of Hebrew vocabulary and biblical language development.

BIBLICAL SCHOLARSHIP

David, Jonathan, and the Books of Samuel

Commentaries and biblical scholarship exploring covenant, kingship, grief, loyalty, and narrative structure within the Davidic account.

First and Second Samuel by Walter Brueggemann

Influential commentary examining political tension, covenant, grief, and theological themes within Samuel.

King David by Jonathan Kirsch

Historical and literary exploration of David’s life, relationships, and royal ascent.

The David Story by Robert Alter

Translation and literary commentary emphasizing narrative artistry and emotional depth within the Hebrew text.

ANCIENT CULTURE

Ancient Near Eastern Culture & Masculinity

Historical resources examining covenant customs, masculinity, grief rituals, royal succession, kinship, and emotional expression within the ancient world.

The New Manners and Customs of Bible Times

Historical background surrounding ancient customs, covenant relationships, and biblical social structures.

Honor, Patronage, Kinship and Purity

Cultural framework analysis for understanding social relationships within biblical societies.

Dictionary of Biblical Imagery

Symbolic and literary imagery resource for covenant, grief, kingship, and emotional language.

THEOLOGY & HISTORY

Interpretation, Translation, and Church History

Sources examining biblical interpretation, theological development, translation history, and hermeneutical traditions across generations.

A History of Biblical Interpretation

Historical overview of interpretive traditions and theological development throughout church history.

How to Read the Bible for All Its Worth

Hermeneutical study resource exploring historical context, genre, interpretation, and translation.

The Journey from Texts to Translations

Study of manuscript transmission, translation philosophy, and biblical textual history.

HEALING & BELONGING

Spiritual Trauma, Identity, and LGBTQIA+ Faith

Resources exploring belonging, spiritual trauma, pastoral care, identity, dignity, and the emotional impact of fear based theology.

Shameless by Nadia Bolz Weber

Exploration of sexuality, faith, belonging, and grace within Christian experience.

God and the Gay Christian by Matthew Vines

Biblical and theological examination of LGBTQIA+ inclusion and interpretation within Christianity.

Walking the Bridgeless Canyon

Reflections on spiritual trauma, faith, identity, and emotional survival within religious environments.

Preserved Voices

Every source listed here represents generations of readers, scholars, translators, pastors, historians, and wounded believers wrestling honestly with sacred text. Some approached Scripture searching for certainty. Others searched for understanding, healing, or belonging. Together, their work reminds us that honest study has always been part of faith itself.
