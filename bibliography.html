Sources & Further Reading

Bibliography

A reader's guide to selected biblical and interpretive sources referenced in the book.

The chapters of A Love Older Than Fear include references relevant to their specific discussions. The works below are selected sources named in the manuscript, not an exhaustive list of every source or interpretive tradition discussed.

The Holy Bible

King James Version. Biblical passages referenced in the manuscript include 1 Samuel 18–20, 2 Samuel 1, Genesis 32, John 20, and Psalms. Consult the references at the end of each chapter for chapter-specific passages.

Brueggemann, Walter

First and Second Samuel. A scholarly commentary on the books of Samuel and their historical, literary, and theological context.

Alison, James

Faith Beyond Resentment. A theological work relevant to questions of faith, interpretation, and human relationships.

Nouwen, Henri

Writings on vulnerability, spiritual belonging, and compassionate faith, as referenced in the manuscript.

For accuracy, consult the References section at the end of each chapter for the sources and biblical passages attached to that chapter. This page should be expanded if additional bibliographic details or works are added to the final manuscript.
