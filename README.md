# A Love Older Than Fear

A Love Older Than Fear is a digital theological and literary project exploring the relationship between David and Jonathan through Scripture, covenant, grief, tenderness, translation history, emotional honesty, and the impact fear has shaped within modern religious interpretation.

This project combines biblical study, historical context, pastoral reflection, literary atmosphere, and compassionate engagement surrounding faith, belonging, masculinity, emotional vulnerability, and LGBTQIA+ spiritual experience.

# Project Vision

This work exists to encourage thoughtful study without fear controlling the conversation before the conversation even begins.

The project explores:

- David and Jonathan
- Covenant theology
- Hebrew language studies
- Ancient Near Eastern culture
- Translation history
- Grief and lament in Scripture
- Emotional vulnerability within sacred text
- Fear based theology
- LGBTQIA+ faith and belonging
- Spiritual healing and church hurt
- Compassion centered biblical reflection

The goal is not panic or outrage.

The goal is honest engagement.

# Features

## Atmospheric Design

The site uses immersive typography, layered gradients, floating particle atmospheres, cinematic spacing, and manuscript inspired layouts to create a contemplative reading experience.

## Study Experience

Readers can explore:
- guided study questions
- theological reflections
- historical resources
- prayer requests
- covenant reflections
- emotional and literary themes

## Prayer Room

Visitors may submit prayer requests through a connected form system. Public requests may be shared manually within the prayer wall while private requests remain confidential.
