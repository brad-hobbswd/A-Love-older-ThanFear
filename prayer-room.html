No Fear Before God

Prayer has never belonged only to the strong, the certain, the unafraid, or the spiritually polished. Prayer belongs to grieving people. Prayer belongs to exhausted people. Prayer belongs to wounded people. Prayer belongs to people searching for breath beneath the weight of fear. God has never feared honesty.
