PURPOSE

Why This Archive Exists

This project exists to encourage thoughtful engagement with Scripture, historical honesty, emotional reflection, and compassionate theological conversation surrounding passages many readers were taught to approach with fear long before approaching them carefully.

The goal of this manuscript is not outrage, division, or sensationalism. The goal is honest study, spiritual reflection, historical awareness, tenderness toward wounded people, and reverence toward sacred text.

Questions surrounding David and Jonathan survived centuries because Scripture itself preserved their story with unusual emotional depth. This archive exists within that continuing conversation.

Preservation Matters

Some stories disappear quietly into history. Others continue surviving because generations keep returning to them searching for meaning, truth, grief, tenderness, belonging, and the face of God somewhere inside the human experience itself.
